eSpeak-NG Afrikaans Compact Engine & Data Pack (GPL-3.0-or-later)
=================================================================
Engine: eSpeak-NG 1.51.1 WebAssembly Compact Synthesizer
Shared Engine & Data Pack Size: ~3.3 MB (3,460,300 bytes)
Worker Architecture: Isolated GPL-3.0-or-later Web Worker (separate from the MIT Piper ONNX neural worker)

Included Afrikaans Tone Variants
--------------------------------
Piper currently lists no Afrikaans model. eSpeak-NG supplies Afrikaans using a ~3.3 MB shared compact engine/data pack:
1. Afrikaans (Female - Synthetic Tone Variant, af+f3)
2. Afrikaans (Male - Synthetic Tone Variant, af+m3)

Both selections are documented synthetic tone variants, labelled as such rather than presented as neural recordings. Both share the same 3.3 MB download.
